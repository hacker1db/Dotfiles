Generate a comprehensive weekly review analyzing the past week's activity, progress, and planning for next week.

## Analysis Sources

**Primary Sources:**
- `0.Quick Notes 📨/0. Inbox Notes.md`
- `1.Projects/Yearly Goals Dashboard 🥅.md`
- `1.Projects/Tasks.md`
- `2.Areas/Personal Home/Journal/` (past 7 days)
- `2.Areas/Work Notes/DevSecOps Notes/` (past 7 days)
- `2.Areas/Personal Home/Blog Posts 🕸/` (draft status)

**Optional (if configured):**
- Readwise highlights (past 7 days)
- NVD CVEs (past 7 days, CVSS >= 8.0)

## Output

Saves to: `2.Areas/Personal Home/Weekly Reviews/YYYY-MM-DD Weekly Review.md`
