# Book Review

Create a sourced local review with critique and connections across the user's reading.

1. Resolve the book from the request. Follow `../readwise-cli/references/access-patterns.md` for authenticated access. Use available capabilities. Read and follow `references/research-workflow.md` before research or drafting every library grounded review.
2. Read `references/output-and-style.md` before writing. Preserve its required thematic structure, critique, further reading, verified source links, length guidance, and separate quality pass.
3. Draft in the user's voice without inventing personal reactions or experiences. Leave `%% TODO: ... %%` for unknown personal details. Ground claims and connections in retrieved evidence; disclose sparse highlights or missing related sources.
4. Save the complete Markdown draft to the requested or project appropriate destination and link it. Honor requested length and format.
5. Publish to Reader only with authorization already present in the conversation. Follow `references/publication.md` when requested. Otherwise present the finished local draft before a publication decision. Report failures and preserve partial work.
