# Research and interactive teaching

## Research

Use the available search and page reader tools; do not require a model or a particular tool name. Read two or three authoritative sources where available. Extract the definition, mechanism, practical uses, and useful diagrams. A simple explanation from a secondary source may help an analogy, but verify factual claims against primary material. Choose one strongest source for Go deeper. Cite the actual sources used.

## Session arc

Offer approximately 30 minutes including the roadmap and optional recall: definition 4 minutes, relevance 4, mechanism 7, example 6, insight 4, quiz 4, and introduction or recall 1. Treat timing as guidance and adapt to user preference.

1. Roadmap: show the six steps. If a related prior session exists, ask one short recall question and give feedback before new material. Wait for that answer.
2. What it is: give one simple sentence, one layer of nuance, and an everyday analogy.
3. Why it matters: use two or three situations from the research. Mention products only where relevant.
4. How it works: use at most five numbered steps. For each, state what happens, connect it to the analogy, and explain why it precedes the next step.
5. Real example: follow a character with a concrete goal through the mechanism. Use the supplied mission if present.
6. Key insight: explain the most useful mental model in two or three sentences. Connect it to the mission when supplied, without claiming an unsupported surprising fact.
7. Quiz: ask exactly three questions, one easy, one medium, and one application question. Interleave a related prior topic when available. For multiple choice, use equal word counts and similar character counts where possible so length does not reveal the answer. Wait for answers, then give specific feedback for each, correcting errors through the analogy.

Use short sentences, translate technical terms immediately, and introduce one concept at a time. Pause for understanding at natural boundaries; if the user requests an uninterrupted lesson, consolidate checks into the quiz. Keep unanswered questions unanswered in saved records.
